## Phase Goal

**As a** trader, **I want to** ask the AI to analyze and trade for me, **so that** I can act through conversation.

## Acceptance Criteria (this plan)

- [ ] After a reply with successful actions has refreshed, the rows the AI changed are briefly highlighted: the position row of a traded ticker and the watchlist row of an added ticker (CHAT-05, highlight half; D-03)
- [ ] Nothing the AI did not successfully change is highlighted, and nothing is highlighted when the refresh failed
