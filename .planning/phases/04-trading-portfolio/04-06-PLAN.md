## Flagged Assumptions

- **`Date.parse` of six-digit fractions (RESEARCH Open Question, assumption A2).** Chromium parses the backend's microsecond timestamps, but Safari and Firefox were not tested, and ECMAScript's date format defines exactly three fraction digits. `snapshotTime` trims to milliseconds with one regex, the simplest option that is correct in every engine, and is unit-tested.
- **The tail is recomputed per render with `Date.now()`.** It is not stored. The chart re-renders on every price tick (`usePriceVersion`) and on history or portfolio changes, which matches the UI-SPEC's "now = Date.now() at the latest price tick or data refresh".
- **No `useMemo` for the downsample.** `downsample` costs at most 300 index reads per render, so the chart calls `buildPnlSeries` directly (no premature optimisation).
- **History payload size (A5, accepted).** A week of 30 s snapshots is about 1.5 MB every 30 s. DATA-V2-01 (server bucketing) is the deferred fix.
