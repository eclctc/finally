## Flagged Assumptions

- **`SPARKLINE_POINTS` lives in `Sparkline.tsx`**, not in the store. RESEARCH sketched it in `priceStore.ts`, but the sparkline is its only reader, and the store owns only the 1,200-point window.
- **Time formatters live in `frontend/src/lib/formatTime.ts`**, not in `format.ts`. `format.ts` holds the E2E-facing number formatters (bare figures the Playwright `parseNumber` rule reads back); time labels are chart-only. Plan 04-05 adds `formatSignedCurrency` to `format.ts`, and Plan 04-06 adds the P&L time formats to `formatTime.ts`.
- **Tooltip behavior in a real browser** (hover position, no clipping at 413px) is a UAT backstop. jsdom cannot drive hover, so `PriceTooltip` is unit-tested as a plain component.
