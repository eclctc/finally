## Phase Goal

**As a** trader, **I want to** chart, buy and sell tickers and see my portfolio update, **so that** I can manage my positions.

## Acceptance Criteria (this plan)

- [ ] The user can buy or sell a quantity, including a fractional one, from the trade bar; buttons are disabled while the request is in flight (TRADE-03)
- [ ] A rejected trade shows the backend error text inline in the trade bar (TRADE-04)
- [ ] After a successful trade the header cash and total update from the trade response with no reload (TRADE-05, cash half; positions are Plan 04-05, history Plan 04-06)
- [ ] The portfolio refreshes every 30 seconds, and a background failure never blanks valid numbers (PORT-04, portfolio half; history half is Plan 04-06)

<objective>
Lift the one portfolio source into `Dashboard` and prove the trade path end to end: the trade bar posts a market order, the response's portfolio replaces the header figures in the same render, and backend rejections show inline. Then complete the trade bar contract and add the 30 s poll with a guard so an older GET can never overwrite a newer trade response.

Purpose: Trading is the core of this phase, and the lifted portfolio is the seam every later panel (positions, heatmap, P&L) and Phase 5 read. Proving it first de-risks the whole phase.
Output: `TradeBar.tsx` + tests, `usePortfolio.ts` (setPortfolio, poll, guard) + tests, props-driven `Header.tsx` with converted tests, `Dashboard.tsx` wiring, shared `errorMessage` in `api.ts`, portfolio fixtures.
</objective>
