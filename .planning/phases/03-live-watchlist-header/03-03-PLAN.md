## Phase Goal

**As a** trader, **I want to** watch live prices stream into my watchlist, **so that** I can follow the market at a glance.

## Acceptance Criteria (this plan)

- [ ] The header dot is green when connected, yellow while reconnecting, red only when the stream is CLOSED, and green again once a recreated stream opens (LIVE-02)
- [ ] The header shows the cash balance (`$10,000.00` on a fresh DB) and a live total portfolio value revalued at live prices (LIVE-08)

<objective>
Make the header live. The connection dot follows the real stream, the total portfolio value is recomputed from live prices, and the Phase 1 findings this work touches are fixed: the dot's accessible name (WR-02), the missing page `h1`, and the double rounding in `format.ts` (WR-01).

Purpose: The header is the user's staleness signal and focal figure. It must never claim a live connection or a value it does not have.
Output: `ConnectionDot.tsx`, `usePortfolio.ts`, `portfolioValue.ts` with tests, an updated `Header.tsx` and its tests, and the WR-01 formatter fix with updated tests.
</objective>
