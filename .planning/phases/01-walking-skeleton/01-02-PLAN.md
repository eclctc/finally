## Flagged Assumptions

- **FND-04 (unclassified edge, unresolved, review manually):** the jsdom stubs are deliberately small doubles. They prove components can render and be driven under jsdom; they do not prove real-browser `EventSource` reconnect or `ResizeObserver` sizing. Real-browser behavior is owned by the Phase 3 spike (`sse-reconnect.spec.ts`) and the Phase 6 Playwright gate.
- **Precision contract note:** cents use `Math.round`, which rounds ties toward +infinity (`0.005` becomes `$0.01`, `-0.005` becomes `$0.00`), while `formatPercent` uses Intl's half-expand rounding. Quantities show at most 4 decimals, so a holding below 0.00005 shares renders as `0`. Both are accepted as the display contract for this project.
- **Prohibition recall breadcrumb (canon, not minted):** rendering backend or LLM text as raw HTML is canon XSS, covered by `/gsd-secure-phase`; Plan 03 adds a grep gate for it.
