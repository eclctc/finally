## Phase Goal

**As a** trader, **I want to** get clean errors and consistent chat actions, **so that** the AI copilot is reliable.

This plan keeps the numbers honest: a ticker the user (or the AI) removes and re-adds keeps its daily change % instead of silently resetting it.

<objective>
Keep each ticker's session open price for the life of the backend, across remove and re-add, and make the simulator resume a re-added ticker at its last price (HARD-07).

Purpose: today `PriceCache.remove` drops the open with the price, so a re-add takes the new price as the open (reproduced: open 110.0 instead of 100.0). A cache-only fix is not enough for the simulator: `GBMSimulator.remove` forgets the price and `add` draws a new random seed for unknown tickers (reproduced: 71.06 then 342.02), which would pair the old open with an unrelated price (about +381%).
Output: `_session_opens` in the cache, `last_prices` in the simulator, and tests for both sources.
</objective>

## Assumptions

- A5 (research): on re-add the simulator resumes at the last simulated price and the original session open is kept. The alternative (restart at the open, change 0%) is a visible reset and was not chosen.
- `_session_opens` keeps one float per ticker ever seen for the backend lifetime. With a single user and a small watchlist this growth is negligible; no pruning is added.
